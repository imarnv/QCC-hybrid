🧊 Tensor-Isomorphism Authentication
Research-Stage PQC
1

Public cube T₀

A random 8×8×8 cube of numbers mod 251, published by everyone.

2

Secret twist (A, B, C)

Three secret invertible matrices mix the cube along its x, y and z axes, giving public cube T₁.

3

Hard problem

Given only T₀ and T₁, finding the twist is the Tensor Isomorphism problem: no known fast classical or quantum algorithm.

4

Zero-knowledge signature

The signer proves they know the twist over 64 challenge rounds without revealing it (Fiat–Shamir).
