# Video Production Prompt — Quantum-Resilient Cryptographic Framework

**Target length:** 2:00 max · **Format:** 16:9, 1080p or 4K, 30 fps
**Structure:** 10 AI-generated 3D clips cut together with 4 short screen recordings of the real dashboard (`python3 app.py` → http://localhost:5001)

> **How to use this file.**
> 1. Paste the **Global Style Block** at the start of every clip prompt, so all the clips look like one film.
> 2. Generate each clip at the length shown, plus 2–3 variations of the hero clips (C5, C6, C7, C8).
> 3. Record the 4 screen inserts (Part 4).
> 4. Edit: clips → voice-over (Part 3, about 275 words) → overlays (Part 5) → music (Part 6).
>
> AI video models garble on-screen text, so every prompt says "no text". Add labels and equations in the editor.

> **Tone:** confident, calm, slightly fast documentary pace. Timestamps show where each line starts.

**[0:00 · C1]** "A large quantum computer breaks the encryption that protects the internet today, and attackers can record our data now to decrypt it later."

**[0:08 · C2]** "So we built a quantum-resilient framework: four layers, each based on a different kind of hardness."

**[0:14 · C3]** "Layer one, BB84 quantum key distribution. Arnav sends photons in random bases; Monesh measures in his own random bases. They keep only the matches. That's a shared secret key."

**[0:24 · C4]** "If Eve listens, physics betrays her: measuring a photon disturbs it. The error rate jumps past eleven percent, and the key is thrown away."

**[0:34 · Screen A]** "In our dashboard: zero percent error without Eve, and the key aborted the moment she appears."

**[0:40 · C5]** "But BB84's public discussion can be faked. So we add a new, research-stage layer: cube cryptography. A public eight-by-eight-by-eight cube of numbers is twisted along all three axes by a secret key."

**[0:52 · C6]** "Undoing that twist is the tensor isomorphism problem, a maze for classical and quantum computers alike. A zero-knowledge signature seals the transcript, and any tampering breaks the seal."

**[1:02 · Screen B]** "Genuine signature accepted. Forgeries rejected."

**[1:07 · C7]** "Layer three: lattices, the maths behind NIST's ML-KEM standard. Hide a secret point in an infinite grid and blur it with noise."

**[1:18 · C8]** "In two dimensions you'd find it instantly. In hundreds of dimensions, not even a quantum computer can. Yet the key holder snaps every noisy bit back into place."

**[1:32 · C9]** "Layer four fuses the keys through HKDF into one AES-256 session key. Lose the quantum layer, and the others still hold."

**[1:40 · Screens C+D]** "Full pipeline, Eve on, and the message still decrypts. Across the network, every link is authenticated."

**[1:48 · C10]** "Physics, geometry, lattices, encryption. Breaking one isn't enough. Defense in depth for the quantum era."
